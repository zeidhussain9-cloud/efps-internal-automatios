# Website Management — Agent Guidance

This module is dedicated exclusively to EasyFind Property Solutions website management and automation.

## Boundary

Keep website-specific business workflows here. Reusable technical capabilities belong under `shared/`.

## Current status

Skeleton only. Do not add implementation until a concrete website capability is required and its source truth is established.

Keep the module name lowercase and hyphenated: `efps-website-mgmnt`.

## Safety

Do not silently redefine website or EFPS business rules, and do not commit credentials.
